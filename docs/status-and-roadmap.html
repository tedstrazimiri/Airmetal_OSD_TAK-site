  • Full original suite: 118 tests passed using retained private fixture inputs.

  • Asset regeneration: matched the retained model assets, catalog and source report.
  • Topology: documentary MATRIX contacts and AM_osd RX/TX endpoint labels checked; browser/bypass/filter/keyboard checks passed.
  • These checks do not accept an installed firmware image, live aircraft telemetry, analog video integrity, radio behavior or flight readiness.

Required implementation gates

  1. Define replayable gateway/display contracts and separate validity for ownship position, aircraft azimuth, markers and link health.
  2. Prove Pi-generated text through independently powered AM_osd on a known single-ended PAL source/monitor.
  3. Measure revision-matched MATRIX levels, common mode, polarity, termination and equalization; obtain a real receive-only capture.
  4. Establish a usable aircraft azimuth path. GPS ground course is insufficient for the proposed heading-up/fan behavior.
  5. Qualify extraction/reinjection and a whole-unit bypass that disconnects both active video adapter ends.
  6. Implement bounded TAK ingest, identity/deletes/expiry, nearest-marker ordering and MGRS.
  7. Port SA primitives/page ownership and coordinate the airborne OSD; prove reset/power-loss/reconnect and goggles readability before installed/flight acceptance.

Camera selection and SA page selection remain independent. No new AUX assignment or payload-control behavior is established by this project. The separate world-down storyboard does not add moving-release trajectory prediction to this prototype.
